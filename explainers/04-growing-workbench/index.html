The Growing Workbench

Chat is where most work with language models starts. It should not be where it has to stay. A history of interfaces that grew with the work, a replay of one real conversation growing into a workbench, and the plan for a first pilot.

References: Carroll and Carrithers, “Training Wheels in a User Interface” (1984). Alexander et al., The Oregon Experiment (1975). Brand, How Buildings Learn (1994). Norton Commander (1986). Borland Turbo Vision (1990). Eclipse perspectives (2001). The replay condenses a real conversation. Pixel sprites are original. Everything runs locally and deterministically.
