## Limits

Fake-model evidence only, not real Mistral dogfood. Files refreshes on page reload,
has no editing/download/binary/large-file support and intentionally skips even
in-root symlinks. Rename uses double-click then Enter/Escape. Panels stack/scroll
on narrow screens. The key store retains M1's documented same-user trust and
cross-process write-race limits; key-paste rejection is heuristic. Host/Origin
headers are required as in M1. Known W0 Linux suite issues are not fixed.
